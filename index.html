Hybrid RAG retrieval system

Question answering over 24 machine-learning papers, 2026

A retrieval-augmented generation service over 2,665 passages. Keyword (BM25) and dense retrieval are fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, then reranked by a cross-encoder. A groundedness gate declines questions the papers can’t answer before the language model is called, and every citation is checked server-side against the passages that were actually retrieved.

Measured on a 40-question evaluation harness (recall@k, MRR, nDCG) across four retriever ablations. 236 tests, all offline.
Hybrid retrievalHit rate 0.971→to 1.000
Cross-encoder rerankingMean reciprocal rank, same recall 0.919→to 0.938
Groundedness gateUnanswerable questions declined 5 of 5, with no false refusals

Built with Python, FastAPI, PyTorch, FAISS and Docker.

Causal deconfounding

Shows that a purely associative deep model latches onto a confounder, so it fails out of distribution and treats patient subgroups unfairly. Adding the IRM invariance objective removes the shortcut and restores both.
